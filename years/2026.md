# 2026 UCI New Venture Competition

[← Back to main archive](../README.md)

**Total Prize Pool:** Over $100,000 (Cash) plus AWS credits (in-kind) for all semi-finalists
**Theme:** Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare and Education Access
**Edition:** 22nd annual

The 2026 Stella Zhang New Venture Competition Grand Finale was held **Thursday, May 21, 2026, 5:30-8:00 PM PT** in the SB1 Auditorium at the UCI Paul Merage School of Business (4293 Pereira Drive, Irvine; Building 222). Ten finalist teams (two per track) pitched in the final round of the seven-month competition. **Pipeline**, an AI transfer-planning platform for community college students, won the $20,000 Grand Prize.

The dominant story of 2026 was **AI**: at least 6 of the 10 finalists were AI-native ventures, clustering into two application areas - cognitive-health / Alzheimer's detection (LAMON and NeuroStrat swept Life Sciences 1st/2nd, and also took the Beall Applied Innovation Award and the overall Runner-Up) and education access (Pipeline and Ascend First, two community-college-transfer platforms, took the Grand Prize and the MIE Award respectively).

## Notes

- **Confirmed cash total: $103,500** (sum of the cash-prize column above, excluding the Beall Applied Innovation Award). UCI framed the pool as "over $100,000"; that figure omits several of the specialty/cross-listed line items counted here. The $10,000 Beall Applied Innovation Award is excluded from this total because Merage frames it as tied to a Wayfinder Incubator and mentorship package rather than a stated cash prize, and no source confirms whether it is paid as cash; the total is $113,500 if it is. AWS website-build credits were awarded in-kind to every semi-finalist and are not counted either way. (An early OC Startup Council recap was rendered by one automated summarizer as a "$93,500" total; that figure is not stated in the article and does not match either line-item sum.)
- **Cross-listings** (a team winning both an overall/specialty award and a track award): Pipeline (Grand Prize + Consumer Services 1st = **$30,000** total), LAMON (Beall Applied Innovation + Life Sciences 1st = $10,000 confirmed, $20,000 if the Beall award is cash), RestWing (Fan Favorite + Consumer Products 1st = $11,000), NeuroStrat (Runner-Up + Life Sciences 2nd = $10,000), Ascend First (MIE Award + Social Enterprise 2nd = $7,500).
- **New specialty award:** The **Beall Applied Innovation Award** ($10,000) is the largest specialty award in the archive and appears for the first time in 2026, awarded to LAMON alongside its Life Sciences 1st placement. LAMON's two UCI MIND-affiliated faculty (Dr. Ahmad Sajjadi, Dr. Pooyan Mobtahej) were recognized in UCI MIND's own coverage of the team's two awards.
- **Judging panel (5):** Isabelle Bart (Founder, Impact Innovator; recurring NVC judge, also 2022 and 2023), Scott Joslyn (UCI Health Ventures), Jasmine J. (last name not published), Matt Sand (co-founder & President, 3DEO), Charla Triplett (Founder, Catalyze; University Lab Partners). See [`SPONSORS-AND-JUDGES.md`](../SPONSORS-AND-JUDGES.md#2026-22nd-annual).
- **Rosters:** Several teams' full rosters remain unpublished (Ascend First, Simly, Abner Computing, BrickCycle). RestWing's likely founder appears to be **Sahil Kulkarni** (UCI; Zotbotics Robotics Club) per LinkedIn search snippets, but this could not be confirmed against a directly fetchable source and is not asserted in the table above. PAQQ's four listed names come only from the PAQQ LinkedIn company page (no roles/affiliations published). The official 2026 Beall Center NVC report PDF is the canonical roster source but is password-protected; see [`Sources & Notes`](../README.md#sources--notes).
- **Naming notes:** Simly is styled "SimlyAI" in the OC Startup Council recap; its product is branded "Simly EHR." Do not confuse it with the unrelated companies *SimCare AI* or *Simbie AI*. BrickCycle (UCI) is unrelated to *BrickCycle Habitats* (Vancouver, BC) and to the Australian "Brick Cycle." Pipeline and Ascend First are two distinct 2026 transfer-focused teams.

## Post-competition outcomes (selected)

All ten 2026 finalists are **too recent** for meaningful outcome data (the Grand Finale was May 21, 2026). Early public footprints:

- **Pipeline** (Grand Prize + Consumer Services 1st): OPERATING (brand new). Product marketed at pipelineplanning.com.
- **LAMON** (Beall Applied Innovation + Life Sciences 1st): OPERATING (research-backed). Built on published UCI Neurology work - Mobtahej et al., *Alzheimer's & Dementia* (2025) on speech-derived digital biomarkers for cognitive-impairment detection.
- **NeuroStrat** (Runner-Up + Life Sciences 2nd): OPERATING (research-backed). Based on Casey Vanderlip's UCI/Stark Lab research combining short digital memory assessments with blood biomarkers to predict cognitive decline.
- **RestWing** (Fan Favorite + Consumer Products 1st): OPERATING (brand new). restwing.com live.
- **PAQQ** (Consumer Products 2nd): OPERATING (brand new). paqqsmart.com live.
- **CuraVoice** (Consumer Services 2nd): OPERATING (brand new). curavoice.com live; profiled earlier in a March 2025 UCI ANTrepreneur AI Innovation Challenge spotlight.
- **Simly** (Business Products & Services 1st): OPERATING (brand new). Simly EHR piloting at the UCI School of Medicine (gated app).
